Our History

A Living Piece
of World War II

The SS Jeremiah O'Brien is the last unaltered Liberty Ship in existence — a floating testament to American industry and the courage of the men and women who built and sailed her into harm's way.

Built in June 1943 at the New England Shipbuilding Corporation in South Portland, Maine in just 56 days, she completed seven wartime voyages including eleven crossings of the English Channel during the D-Day invasion of Normandy — more Channel crossings than any other vessel.

In 1994, the O'Brien made the extraordinary 18,000-mile round trip back to Normandy for the 50th anniversary commemoration — the only surviving Liberty Ship to do so. Today she is fully certified by the US Coast Guard and approved by the American Bureau of Shipping, operated by an all-volunteer crew since 1979.
